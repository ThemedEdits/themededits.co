Freelance Graphic and Web Designer

Hi, I'm Hammad.
Let's build something good.

I'm Hammad Ahmed, a freelance graphic designer and web developer based in Karachi, Pakistan, working directly with clients worldwide through Themed Edits. I have four years of experience in graphic design and one year in web development, with more than 40 projects delivered so far. I started out designing social posters and logos, then moved into web design and development, allowing me to handle both the visual and technical sides of a project myself. I care about clean typography, deliberate motion and interfaces that feel considered rather than templated. Whether it's a brand identity, a social design or a complete website, I like to understand the bigger picture and build the work around it.
