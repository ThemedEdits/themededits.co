Studio

Hi, I'm the person
you'll be working with.

I'm Hammad Ahmed, the founder of Themed Edits, a one-person studio based in Karachi, Pakistan, offering graphic design and web development for clients worldwide. I have four years of experience in graphic design and one year in web development, and I've delivered more than 40 projects. I started out designing social posters and logos, picked up front-to-back development along the way, and now handle both sides of a project myself: the visual system and the site that carries it. That means no brief gets lost in translation between a designer and a developer, because it's the same person holding both. I care about clean type, deliberate motion and interfaces that feel considered rather than templated, and I bring that same care to every logo, poster and product I ship. If you're looking for someone to think through the whole problem, not just one slice of it, that's the work I do.
